    How It Works

    A fast FPGA clock runs continuously and is divided down by a clock divider circuit to create a precise 1-second pulse. This pulse drives counters that store the current time in minutes and seconds. The values from the counters are then sent to a display driver, which lights up segments of a 7-segment display to show the time. This simulation emulates that hardware concept interacting in your browser.
